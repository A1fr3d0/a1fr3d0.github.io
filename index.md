## 💪 Usage

Once the app is open in your browser, you can:

- **View Your Workout Plan:** See a clear list of daily exercises and routines.
- **Track Progress:** Mark exercises as completed to see your daily progress.
- **Manage Routines:** Add, edit, or remove workouts as your fitness goals change.
- **Stay Organized:** All your fitness guidance is in one place, easy to find and follow.

The app is designed for simplicity. Just click on buttons and links to navigate. Your data is stored locally on your computer, so it stays private.

## ⚙️ Configuration

The app works out of the box with no setup required. However, you can customize it:

- **Change Workout Days:** Edit the `index.html` file with a text editor (like Notepad) to modify the default routine.
- **Adjust Exercises:** Add or remove exercises by editing the simple list in the HTML.
- **Reset Data:** Clear your browser's local storage to start fresh.

To edit the app, open `index.html` in Notepad or any text editor. Look for the section labeled "Workout Data" and change the text as needed. Save the file and refresh your browser to see updates.

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Do I need to be online to use the app?**
A: No. Once you download and open the file, it works completely offline.

**Q: Will my workouts be saved?**
A: Yes, the app saves your progress in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data will erase it.

**Q: Can I use this on my phone?**
A: Yes, but the app is optimized for desktop screens. It will work on a phone browser, but may look smaller.

**Q: How do I update the app?**
A: Download the latest version from the GitHub link and replace your old `index.html` file.

**Q: Is my data shared with anyone?**
A: No. Everything stays on your computer. The app does not send any data to servers.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

**App does not open:**
- Make sure you double-click `index.html` and not another file.
- Try using a different web browser.

**Exercises are not saving:**
- Check that your browser allows local storage (it is enabled by default).
- Do not use private/incognito mode for persistent saving.

**Page looks broken:**
- Try refreshing the page (press F5 or Ctrl+R).
- Update your browser to the latest version.
